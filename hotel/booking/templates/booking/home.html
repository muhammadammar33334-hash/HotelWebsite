About Amin Kabab House

Amin Kabab House is a traditional food destination in Mirpur, Azad Kashmir, known for BBQ and desi food.

The official menu includes popular dishes such as Mutton Seekh Kabab, Chicken Seekh Kabab, Mutton Seekh Tikka, Chicken Malai Boti, Mutton Karahi, Mutton Kaleji Handi, Chicken Handi Boneless and Chicken Karahi.
